## Lessons learned the hard way (apply them as kill tests)
1. FREE-FIX KILL: if the responsible party can fix the problem in a day with free data or one setting, nobody pays. We need pains whose fix needs sustained LABOUR, LANGUAGE or JUDGEMENT the payer lacks.
2. PAYER-PENALTY TEST: the party that suffers the deadline/penalty must be the one we sell to (in the UAE the penalty hits suppliers, not the providers we wanted to sell to).
3. DINAR TRAP: individual gig work pays out in DZD (Stripe/Wise to Algeria). Only B2B invoices settled by SWIFT in EUR/USD (or pegged currencies) count.
4. REFERENCE BARRIER: tenders select on past references; a newcomer must enter as subcontractor.
5. LEGAL ROLES: one role per client where independence rules exist (e.g. CBAM verifier vs data preparer).
6. Prefer an ORIGINAL MEASUREMENT on public data that sizes the pain and names who bears it.
